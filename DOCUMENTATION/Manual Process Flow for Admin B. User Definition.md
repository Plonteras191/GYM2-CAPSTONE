A. Process Definition
Double Alpha Fitness Gym currently uses a manual process for member registration, attendance recording, membership management, fitness program monitoring, payment recording, and report preparation. The member provides the required information to the gym administrator, who reviews and records the information in the appropriate records. Membership and payment details are also recorded manually. During gym visits, attendance and membership status are checked and recorded by the administrator. For fitness programs, the administrator assigns exercises, observes the member while performing the exercises, and manually records progress. The records are then maintained for monitoring and report preparation.
Figure 4.1. Presents the manual process flow of the gym. The process shows how member information is submitted, recorded, and maintained, followed by attendance, membership checking, fitness program monitoring, and report preparation. Since these activities rely on manual records and checking, retrieving, updating, and maintaining information may require additional time and may result in incomplete or inconsistent records. These identified processes and problems served as the basis for the requirements of the proposed system.

B. User Definition
There is one (1) user of the system, namely the Admin. The Admin is responsible for managing the overall operations of the gym through the system.
•	Admin – The Admin manages member registration, facial data enrollment, attendance monitoring through facial recognition, membership records and renewal, payment transaction recording, fitness program assignment, gesture-based program monitoring, and report generation. The Admin also performs the coaching-related activities, including assigning fitness programs, explaining exercises, and monitoring member progress.
